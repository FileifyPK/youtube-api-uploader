Privacy Policy — Sacred Knowledge Uploads

Effective date: 20 November 2026.

What this app does

Sacred Knowledge Uploads is a personal-use application owned and operated by the owner of the Sacred Knowledge YouTube channel. It uploads videos to that channel — and only that channel — using the YouTube Data API v3, and manages those videos' captions, thumbnails, and playlists. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests exactly two OAuth permissions (scopes):

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — upload videos and set thumbnails on the owner's own channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl — manage captions, playlists, and video metadata on the owner's own channel.

The app does not request access to any other Google service or data. It does not read the owner's emails, files, contacts, or any other YouTube channel.

How data is stored and used

The app stores OAuth tokens only on the owner's own systems, in secure local storage, so it can act on the owner's behalf after a one-time sign-in. Video files, titles, descriptions, captions, and thumbnails handled by the app are the owner's own content, uploaded to the owner's own channel. No personal data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data retention and revocation

Tokens remain stored until the owner revokes access (Google Account → Security → Third-party access) or deletes them. Revoking access immediately stops the app from acting on the channel. The owner may request deletion of any locally stored token or job record at any time by contacting the address below.
